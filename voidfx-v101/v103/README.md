# FOREVER — Dexter × Foo Fighters "Everlong" (voidfx v103, 9:16)

Studio 2. Built on the v9 engine (scaffolded by `tools/new_edit.py`), with the approved v9 text style (keyline captions and a per-letter hero title).

## Pre-render check (handbook §5a), written before the first render

**1. A concept rooted in Dexter's world.** *"I don't have feelings about anything."*
- The one place anything feels real to him is the kill, and he keeps every one forever: a drop of blood on a glass slide. When it's over, the Gulf Stream keeps the rest.
- The song's hook, *"if everything could ever feel this real forever"*, answers his own line.

**2. Three multi-stage, Dexter-themed showpieces:**
- **The drop: THE SAMPLE.** A scalpel slides into the kill-room shot and draws his cheek cut across the frame, as if the picture were skin. Blood beads well up along the cut. On the drop, a glass slide slams onto the lens and squashes the blood into a viscous smear. Then it lifts away and the kill room explodes out of the red.
- **The hook: THE ARCHIVE.** He holds a blood slide up to the light. The camera pushes into the drop, through the red, and comes out flying down an endless corridor of his slides, standing in rows into the dark (*"…feel this real forever"*). One slide rushes up to the lens, and through it is the boat.
- **The loop: THE GULF STREAM.** The bag goes over the side. Splash. The camera follows it under: bubbles, light shafts and the bag sinking into the black, and the song sinks with it. The edit opens in the same water, rising toward the surface, where Dexter stands on the boat above, seen from below the waterline. Then it breaks the surface.

**3. Designed text and graphics in every section:**
- **Cold open:** marine snow and caustic water light; the VO caption I DON'T HAVE FEELINGS / ABOUT **ANYTHING.**; the lamp; kill-room plastic; the whispered BREATHE OUT, / SO I CAN BREATHE YOU IN.
- **Drop:** scalpel, blood beads, slide and smear; the hero title EXCEPT / **THIS.**, built per letter and flooding red; it exits as blood droplets.
- **Montage:** blood-spatter hits on the downbeats; a duct-tape strip; a sea-spray wash.
- **Wonder:** the AND I **WONDER** caption with rain on the glass.
- **Hook:** the slide archive world; the IF EVERYTHING COULD EVER / FEEL THIS **REAL** captions; the hero title **FOREVER**, which exits as rising bubbles as it goes under.

**4. Written comparison with WARMPOP (v6), HIM ALL ALONG (v7) and WISH YOU WELL (v8).**
- **Opening:**
  - v6: a VO over a pastel boat.
  - v7: an eye in a magnifier.
  - v8: a push into a rear-view mirror.
  - **v103:** the edit *starts underwater* and surfaces under Dexter's boat. It's a moving, physical world from frame 1 and part of the loop itself.
- **Hook moment:**
  - v6: the neon end words.
  - v7: HIM ALL ALONG in luminol on plastic.
  - v8: syllable cuts on I DON'T WISH YOU WELL.
  - **v103:** a 3-D flight through an endless archive of his blood slides, with FOREVER built per letter. It's a built world, not a 2-D overlay.
- **Drop:**
  - v6: the CRT dive.
  - v7: the plastic burst.
  - v8: blood blooming in water.
  - **v103:** the sample ritual itself (scalpel, beads, slide press), timed so the slide hits on the song's biggest return.
- **Text:** the approved v9 style throughout (the owner called it perfect). v103 adds a narrative text beat: his VO "I don't have feelings about anything" is answered on the drop by EXCEPT **THIS.**
- **Ending and loop:**
  - v6: a tape rewind.
  - v7: a pupil dive.
  - v8: back into the mirror.
  - v9: through the drop into the moon.
  - **v103:** a burial at sea that *is* the loop. The sinking end and the rising start happen in the same water, with the song muffling under the surface across the wrap.
- **Weakest point of the plan, and the response:** the montage between the drop and the vocal is mostly reused liked basics. **Answer:** it's kept short (3 bars plus a 2-bar splice, under 7 s), it's dense (a move every 4 half-beats), and it carries the new duct-tape and sea-spray transitions and blood-spatter graphics, so the three showpieces stay the substance.

**5. Ambition in review.** Every review pass names the weakest moment and the change that would make it unforgettable (see the review log below).

## Review log

Each pass asked "what would make this unforgettable?", not just "what's broken?".

**Stills and every-cut sheets:**
1. The surfacing showed a hard white line across his face. It's now a thick refracting meniscus, with water droplets on the lens just after the break that flip what's behind them.
2. THE SAMPLE's slide was a huge, barely visible glass and the blood looked like an opaque sticker. The slide is now a framed microscope slide fully on screen, the beads are glossy and some run, and the smear is a liquid film with viscous fingers and a bright meniscus.
3. The splash was a flat crown in mid-air. It's now white water erupting from below the frame.
4. The blinds shot was nearly black, so it was swapped for the red "looks up" shot.
5. A red light-sweep over the dark table shot was replaced with the S1 kill-table shot.
6. **Weakest moment (unforgettable pass): the first quarter-second was black.** He now rises into view through the water within about 4 frames, while frame 0 stays deep so the loop seam matches.
7. **Weakest moment (unforgettable pass): the duct tape read as flat grey bars.** It now has silver sheen, weave and wrinkles.
8. The sea-spray wave drew diagonal stripes, then a zig-zag tear. It's now a rolling, refracting water front with foam and droplets left on the lens.

## Revision 2 (owner notes)

The owner's notes, all addressed in one pass:

1. **"The last word Dexter says … gets cut off."**
   - The audio wasn't truncated: "anything" ends at 53.86 and "but" starts at 53.94. The problem is that he says it mid-sentence with a rising comma inflection, so it sounds cut off however it's faded.
   - **Replaced the line** with *"How is it I can kill people and feel no regret?"* (S4 pack p9, 212.84–215.30). It ends on a natural question fall with 2.6 s of silence after it, and it's played as voice-over, since on its own shot he isn't speaking.
   - The concept stays the same, and the new line ties into the song's *"feel this real"*.
2. **"One of the scenes in the beginning … was really low quality."**
   - Measured and checked at full resolution: the p7 gloving shot was muddy, noisy and soft, and the p8 kill-table shot was also soft.
   - Both were **replaced with sharp shots**: the green-lit stare (p8 152.15, desaturated into the steel palette), the overhead kill table under the lamp (p8 31.70) and the kitchen stare (p8 111.25).
3. **"The subtitles went along with the song but the song didn't sing the lyrics at those parts."**
   - Two text moments had no audible singing:
     - *EXCEPT THIS.* (a designed title on the instrumental drop);
     - the *BREATHE OUT* captions (a whisper that's barely audible; Whisper can't even verify the words on the isolated vocal).
   - Both are **removed**. Text now appears **only on clearly spoken or sung words**, each word on its time: Dexter's line; *"And I wonder"*; *"If everything could ever feel this real"*; **FOREVER**.
4. **"More transitions, animations and graphics … very little dead space."**
   - **Shots:** 19 instead of 15. The edit starts one bar later in the song (1.5 s less quiet lead-in), and every hold is 1.9 s or shorter.
   - **Transitions:** 20 designed transitions. The new ones:
     - **ripple**: a drop of blood falls into the frame and the rings open the next shot;
     - **bubble surge**: bubbles sweep up the frame;
     - the v7 **body-bag zipper**;
     - the v8 **vertical whip** up to the moon.
   - **Graphics:**
     - glass blood slides drifting in soft focus along the edges through the cold open, foreshadowing the archive;
     - a red **heartbeat pulse** on every beat of the build, with a heartbeat underneath;
     - **blood runs down from the top edge** on the riff's off-beats, between the spatter hits;
     - a glow on the moon.
   - All of these are kept off the face.

## Revision 3 (owner notes)

1. **"The blood slides stick around in the beginning."** The drifting glass slides along the edges of the cold open are gone, along with their glass-ticking sounds. The slides now appear only where they belong to the story: the slide he holds up and THE ARCHIVE.
2. **"The random slice and cut with the knife towards the middle … replace that animation."** The knife pass at 11.0 s (a giant blade sweeping across the lens) is replaced by a **glass shatter**, which ties back to the slides:
   - a crack star hits low on the left, by his shoulder, and thin cracks race out across the frame with a slight refraction;
   - on the downbeat the picture breaks into shards that blow outward (nearer shards first), spin, fall and fade, revealing the next shot with a faint red mist;
   - sound: a glass clack, a crackle, the impact and falling tinkles.
   - The first version drew a heavy white web over his face, so the edges are now thin and fade out within about 500 px of the impact.
3. **"Get rid of the random blood dripping animations."** The blood runs from the top edge on the riff's off-beats are gone, with their drip sounds.
4. **Other change:** the blood-spatter hits on the riff's downbeats are also gone. They were random blood too, and one landed on his jaw. The montage keeps its designed transitions (THE SAMPLE, impact, glass shatter, whip, duct tape, logo, zipper) and the beat-cut shots.
