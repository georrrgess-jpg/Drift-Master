# v110 SLEEPWALKER: Dexter × "Sleepwalker (Slowed)" (voidfx, 9:16)

A Dexter edit from the Dexter studio, scaffolded from the v109 PHANTOM standard (`--base v109`) on `dev/pipeline-v2`.

## The brief
*"I'd like for you to make a text edit using the song attached above. I want this edit to be the best text edit you've
ever created. I would like for you to try to focus on the most popular part of the song, or roughly the 49-second mark
and onward. I want this edit to include any custom graphics, animations, and transitions. Try to make it the highest
quality edit you can make."* Asked whether "text edit" meant a Dexter edit (voice-to-text), the owner chose **a Dexter
edit**.

## Concept
**SLEEPWALKER.** His waking life is a sleepwalk, and the nightmare that made him (his mother in the shipping container)
loops forever. The song's line is *"I'm losing power"* and it really does lose power: the bass drops out for a bar and a
half before the drop.
- He lies awake (frame 0). A car's light crosses the bed through the window; he walks through the night half asleep.
- **POWER FAILURE:** the tube above him blinks on with *"I'm"*; **I'M LOSING POWER** lights word by word like a
  fluorescent tube; the mains dip on each word; the brownout kills the letters one by one and the room goes dark into the
  fog. He falls asleep (his eyes close in the fog, his head goes back, the picture drifts down out of focus).
- **THE SURGE** (the drop, the signature): in the fan room the light behind him flickers brown through the turning
  blades while the bass is gone (their light and shadow sweep the room and his shirt, never his face); in the last bar
  the fan spins up and the light flares; on the drop one blade sweeps right across the lens and behind it is the kill
  room (the Dark Passenger awake), the blades' shadows still sweeping it.
- The hunt: Brian's table, the plastic (double vision, drugged), the green screen, the drive (the wiper), the bamboo.
- **THE BARREL** (the focal point): he raises the pistol through the bamboo until it points down the lens; the camera
  pushes into the real muzzle, a light grows at the end of the barrel, and it bursts out of the bore and fills the frame
  as his stare.
- The power fails again (the flicker), an after-image, the red screen developing like a darkroom print.
- **THE SPATTER** (into the blood room, the child's *"Mommy?"*): the blood room's own wall spatter lands over him along
  the arc of a swing, and the room seeps in outward from the blood.
- **THE FALL** (the loop): on the second *"Mommy?"* the blood room drops away above us, light streaks and drops of blood
  rush past, and frame 0 rises from below the whole way, melting into its own out-of-focus light, until we land in it: he
  is awake again.

## Revision 1 (the owner's notes)
*"I like the general style of this edit … I don't like the splatter transition at the end. I would like for you to make
it smoother and more seamless … the loop is really poor … completely change the loop and take a different avenue … I'm
losing power text. I like it, but I don't really like how it appears … incorporate it as more of a beat drop … most of
the transitions right now are kind of lackluster … I also don't really like the doorway transition that appears at 16
seconds, and I don't really like the lines transition at 17 seconds."*
- **I'M LOSING POWER is the drop now.** It leaves the break (the tube still blinks on with "I'm", the room still dips on
  each sung word) and slams on with the power surge on the drop (9.1 s), as the fan blade crosses the lens: white-hot,
  scaled in with motion blur, a shock ripple through the picture, a surge on each beat of the bar, then it loses power
  letter by letter (to 10.4 s).
- **THE SPATTER, smooth:** the cast-off lands softly along the swing, the blood room seeps in from its blood with a
  wide, soft front, and one continuous push runs through the cut; no hard edges, no seam.
- **The loop, THE DRIP:** as he looks up, one lit drop of blood falls in the dark beside his head, lands, and the blood
  room becomes a liquid surface; the ripples run out from the impact and the bed comes up through them as through water,
  calming on frame 0. (A neon outline of frame 0 was tried and dropped: the top-down matte gave a squiggle or a ring
  around his face.)
- **New, custom transitions:** BOKEH (the marina's city lights fall into soft discs and the doorway's lamp comes back
  into focus out of them), TEAR (the drive is a print that tears down one side and is pulled away, his face whole on its
  piece), CURTAIN (the sheer curtain behind him in the doorway shot blows across the lens; replaces the 16 s after-image),
  PLASTIC ROLL (kill-room plastic unrolls down over the lens into the kill room; replaces the 17 s frost), BRUSH (two
  dry-brush strokes paint the cabin in; replaces the slices), and the bamboo fronds into the barrel (for a dolly).
- **Sound:** the title's tubes ignite on the drop, the print tears, the drip lands (the loop's whoosh is gone).

## Revision 2 (the owner's notes)
*"I do like the general theme of this edit … the beginning window graphic at the start of the edit isn't really
necessary … I'm losing power … phase it in. So it would be like, I'm losing, and then power would be when it drops. And
the text for I'm losing would already be written … the blood spatter scene near the end of the edit still isn't quite
adequate … smoother or more cinematic … improve the gun transition to be more seamless and smoother … the beginning of
the beat drop, the scenes shake a little bit too much … reduce the shaking/beat jump."*
- **The window graphic is gone:** the bed → the yard is a gentle push.
- **I'M LOSING POWER, phased:** "I'M LOSING" is written into the dark of the fan room during the last bar of the break,
  letter by letter on the 16th grid, like weak tubes catching (dim, flickering with the fan's low power), so it is already
  there when the drop comes; on the drop "POWER" slams on with the surge and the line comes up to full power.
- **The blood room, cinematic:** THE SWINGING LIGHT: the bare bulb's pool of light swings across him; on its last swing
  the pool grows toward us with the blood-spattered wall lit inside it (a calm shot of him in front of the wall, dmx
  111.8 s), the dark falling away; then a motion-blurred tilt up to him looking at the blood on the ceiling.
- **The barrel, seamless:** one continuous push into the muzzle; a soft light grows in the bore; his stare opens inside it
  through a wide feathered edge and keeps the same forward motion as it settles. No ring, no burst.
- **A calmer drop:** no impact shake on the drop (or before the loop); the beat camera rests through the drop's first bar
  (the title) and runs at half its punch and kick after it.
