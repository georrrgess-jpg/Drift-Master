## The brief

## Concept
**PHANTOM.** *"Rocked out like a phantom."* The Bay Harbor Butcher is a ghost the city hunts and never sees.
- He walks out of the dark (frame 0); a flashlight finds him; on the drop (2.57 s) he **materialises** out of mist in the
  red kill room (the signature).
- On "phantom" the title **PHANTOM** condenses out of mist letter by letter over his double in a reflection, and the
  picture breaks into its own particles.
- The hook is his ghost work: after-images, a glass pane, the phone glitching on "tantrum", the light failing (flicker),
  the headspin, the plastic peel, the evidence photo, the city's surveillance feed, the vanish where he stands, the
  newspaper hunting him (page turn), the night hunt, Doakes ("if you talk shit"), the hammer.
- **The loop:** on "action" he dissolves into mist where he stands; the mist fills the lens and clears on frame 0, where he
  walks out of the dark again.
