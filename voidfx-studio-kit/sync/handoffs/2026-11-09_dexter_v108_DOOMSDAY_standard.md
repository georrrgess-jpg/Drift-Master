## 3. Creative process
- **The window** came from the full-song transcription: the hook names the womb and the brother, which is Dexter's origin and the S1 finale. The song is loop-based with no dynamic drop at the hook, so the studio **built one**: the drums and bass drop out for half a bar under "turn H2O to cognac" and slam back on "DOOMSDAY" with SPECIMEN (the board's "best for the main beat drop").
- **The footage** was chosen to land on the words: the S1 container scene (CBAN 3489) on "ever since the womb"; Dexter strapped and wrapped on Brian's table on "back where my brother went"; lying face up on "my tomb"; Miami Metro and the badge on "government"; the woods with the axe on "unmarked"; the darkroom into the red desk on "I wrote this one"; the trash bags on the boat on "go get bagged".
- **The boards:**
  - SURFACE's TENSION → PEEL → RELEASE maps onto calm → threat, with the grade switching at the peel.
  - SPECIMEN's ENTER → CROSS → REVEAL maps onto the real blood-slide shot → the slide over his eye → the green-lit stare.
- **No text** let the picture carry it: the concepts and the custom transitions do the work that captions did in v107.

## 7. New rules (adopt in both studios)
1. **Even more transitions: aim for about 24–26 designed transitions in a 22 s edit (≥ 1.1/s), nearly all custom and motivated, and keep generic connectors to a handful.** Evidence: rev 1 *"add a lot more transition and unique graphics with custom animated transitions"* (18 → 21 + the loop), then the verdict *"could have contained slightly more transitions/animations"*. Applies to: all edits.
2. **When the owner sends a concept board, build it from a real object in the shot**, not a CG copy next to it (lift, tilt or move the real prop; fill where it was). Evidence: rev 1 *"merge that slide graphic … with one of the actual slides from that scene"*, then the standard. Applies to: all edits.
3. **No droplets on the lens and no wavy heat-haze warps as transitions.** `rainfocus` and `haze108` are now rejected. Evidence: rev 2 *"remove the water droplet filter"*, *"the squiggle, swirl, weird animation"*. Applies to: all edits (the v8 "rain focus pull" like is superseded).
4. **The beat camera is the default, but the owner may turn it off for one edit.** In a slow, menacing edit, consider it. Evidence: rev 1 *"remove that just for this edit. This isn't a complaint that I normally have"*. Applies to: all edits (an exception, not a rule).
5. **Confirmed:** the owner's concept boards and text choices are per edit: *"this is not a new preference or something I want you to institute often"*. Ask when a brief leaves the rest of the edit's text open (one question saved a round here).
6. **Confirmed:** smooth, motivated physical transitions flow. A foreground pass (fronds), dye in water, a darkroom print, luminol, a face match on the eye line and a water surface all stayed.

## 9. For the other studio
- **The density note applies to you too:** the owner asked for more transitions, then said "slightly more" at 0.97/s. Plan for 24+ in a 22 s edit.
- **`rainfocus` and the heat-haze warp are rejected.** v11's `heat_haze` (zigzag fire) is a different piece, but check it on stills before using it in a Dexter-style edit.
- **Render speed (your current work):** registering a new transition in `TR2` makes change tracking re-render every frame, because `render` reads `TR2`. A one-transition swap (rev 2) cost a full render. Tracking which `TR2` keys a frame reads would make swaps partial.
- **The concept-board pattern:** the owner now sends concept boards with timing and sound notes. Build them literally, from the shot's own props, and ask about text when it's left open.
