## 3. Creative process
- **The song's structure carries the concept.** The lyric "I'm losing power" sits right before the bass drops out, so the picture loses power with it and the drop is the power surging back. The fan room (S2, a fan behind him throwing light) gave a motivated light for the drop.
- **The shots** were picked for sleep and night: in bed (frame 0), the night walk, the tube light above him, the fog, eyes closing, then the hunt. The child's "Mommy?" is answered by the blood room (his mother's murder).
- **Before the first delivery:**
  - **Review render:** a drawn fan disc, a black-void page fold, and library transitions that black out the frame (zipper, shutter, eye dive).
  - **The critique:** the barrel's ring on his face, a static drop, a card loop, four look-alike close-ups, target-like rings, a black lower third.
  - All of these were rebuilt before the owner saw it.

## 4. Revision history
- **First delivery** (QC SHIP; 2 full renders).
- **Rev 1, the owner:** *"I like the general style of this edit … I don't like the splatter transition at the end … smoother and more seamless … the loop is really poor … completely change the loop and take a different avenue … I'm losing power text. I like it, but I don't really like how it appears … incorporate it as more of a beat drop … most of the transitions right now are kind of lackluster … I also don't really like the doorway transition that appears at 16 seconds, and I don't really like the lines transition at 17 seconds."*
  - **Change:**
    - The title moved to the drop (a slam with a shock ripple).
    - The spatter got a soft seep.
    - **THE DRIP** loop.
    - New custom transitions: BOKEH, TEAR, CURTAIN (for the 16 s after-image), PLASTIC ROLL (for the 17 s frost), BRUSH and fronds.
  - **Tried and failed:** a neon outline of frame 0 for the loop (the top-down matte gave a squiggle or a ring around his face); the curtain's first folds read as stripes; the plastic's crinkle was harsh noise; the brush left a red rim line across his face.
- **Rev 2, the owner:** *"the beginning window graphic … isn't really necessary … phase it in. So it would be like, I'm losing, and then power would be when it drops. And the text for I'm losing would already be written … the blood spatter scene … still isn't quite adequate … smoother or more cinematic … improve the gun transition to be more seamless and smoother … the beginning of the beat drop, the scenes shake a little bit too much … reduce the shaking/beat jump."*
  - **Change:**
    - The window graphic became a gentle push.
    - The title was phased: "I'M LOSING" written letter by letter into the dark of the fan room during the last bar of the break, "POWER" slamming on with the drop.
    - **THE SWINGING LIGHT** into a calm shot of him in front of the blood wall, then a tilt up to the blood.
    - **THE BARREL** made seamless: a continuous push, light first, a feathered opening.
    - A calmer drop: no impact shake, the camera rests through the drop bar, half the punch after.
  - **Tried and failed / caught:**
    - The bore and the light pool showed the next face (a mouth in the gun, blood red on his lips) while small. Now they show light first.
    - A luma dissolve double-exposed two faces, so it became a tilt.
    - Calming the camera left the bulb shot near still, so the bulb now swings wider.
    - **An appended fix had not taken effect:** `TR2` kept the first-defined function. Now re-registered.
- **Rev 3 = the verdict:** the title removed, then *"you can make the edit as standard."*

  - The spatter took two rounds and was replaced outright.
  - The loop needed a full rethink.
  - Three renders in rev 2.

## 7. New rules (adopt in both studios)
1. **No graphic where the cut needs none.** Evidence: *"the beginning window graphic at the start of the edit isn't really necessary"* (rev 2). Applies to: all edits.
2. **No stripe or line transitions, and no after-images, in a dark, smooth edit.** Evidence: *"the doorway transition that appears at 16 seconds … the lines transition at 17 seconds"* (rev 1; an after-image and a rime frost). Applies to: all edits.
3. **Carry a violent scene in with motivated light, smooth and soft-edged, not a drawn spatter sweep.** Evidence: the spatter rejected twice (*"smoother and more seamless"*, *"smoother or more cinematic"*), then THE SWINGING LIGHT and the verdict. Applies to: Dexter (Flash: a lightning or Speed Force light carrying the scene).
4. **A calmer drop:** no impact shake; the beat camera rests through the drop's first bar and runs at about half its punch and kick after it. Evidence: *"the scenes shake a little bit too much … reduce the shaking/beat jump"* (rev 2). This refines v108 rule 4: the beat camera stays the default, lighter. Applies to: all edits.
5. **Text: start without. If a title rides a drop, phase it** (the setup words already written, the payoff word on the drop), **and expect it to be removed.** Evidence: rev 2's phase-in request, then *"remove the I'm losing power"* at the verdict; the v12 pattern (both titles removed). Applies to: all edits.
6. **A prop dive is one continuous, seamless move:** a push into the real prop, light first, the next face only once the opening is big, the motion carried through the cut. Evidence: *"improve the gun transition to be more seamless and smoother"* (rev 2). Applies to: all edits (Flash: the emblem dive).
7. **The loop is a physical event that lands in frame 0, never a card rising out of the dark.** Evidence: *"the loop is really poor … completely change the loop and take a different avenue"* (rev 1), then THE DRIP stayed. Applies to: all edits.
8. **Technical:**
   - an override appended to `fxN.py` must re-register its `TR2` entry, because the dict keeps the first function;
   - shift a picture with a face in it with edge replication, never reflection (a mirrored second face);
   - check a pack shot's first frames for baked-in ghosting (`dexter_s2_b` 259.6–259.8 s);
   - a pool or opening that holds the next shot shows light first and the next face only once it is big.

   Applies to: all edits.
